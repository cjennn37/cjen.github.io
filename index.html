Alongside my thesis, I have orchestrated several projects leveraging predictive models for computer vision, natural language processing, and bioinformatics. On the side, I regularly conduct quantitative analysis on large datasets tied to my hobbies. For a detailed list of my work, refer to my portfolio.

When I'm not analyzing data professionally, I'm usually applying the same analytical rigour to my passions, whether that's long-distance running, tennis, piano, or chess. Feel free to ask me how I track and model my own performance data for each of them, I'm always down to talk about stats!
